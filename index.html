What exactly are broccoli sprouts?

They're young broccoli plants shortly after germination, with tender stems and tiny seed leaves. They're different from mature broccoli heads and from larger microgreens.

How are they grown?

I grow sprouts in small batches using my growing process, allowing at least six days for broccoli and planning each harvest around confirmed orders.

How do I receive them?

Choose free collection at an agreed time or £2.30 personal London delivery on an available Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday.

How should I store them?

Keep sprouts refrigerated and follow the storage and use-by information supplied with your order. Rinse or cook as appropriate to your preferences and food-safety needs.

Are subscriptions available?

You can enquire about weekly or fortnightly arrangements. Automatic recurring charges are not active yet.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We're here to help.

How soon can I receive my sprouts?

Broccoli needs at least six days to grow. Please request at least seven days ahead; availability and timing are confirmed personally.

Can I collect on any day?

You can request collection any day of the week, subject to an available and agreed collection window. We share the collection details privately.

When do you deliver in London?

Personal delivery is usually possible on Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday in selected postcode areas. The delivery fee is £2.30 per order.

Can I subscribe?

Weekly or fortnightly requests are welcome, but for now these are manual arrangements, not automatic recurring subscriptions.

Do you take payment online?

Not yet. The site prepares a WhatsApp enquiry, and we agree the order and payment arrangements directly before anything is confirmed.

Are sprouts the same as microgreens?

No. MicroSprouts sells young broccoli sprouts, not the larger microgreens harvested later.
