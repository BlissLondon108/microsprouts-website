01 Glucoraphanin
Original research

Broccoli sprouts: an exceptionally rich source of inducers of enzymes that protect against chemical carcinogens

Fahey, Zhang & Talalay · Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences · 1997

This study reported that certain three-day-old broccoli and other cruciferous sprouts contained 10–100 times more glucoraphanin than the corresponding mature plants. The researchers also examined enzyme activity and tumour outcomes in an animal model.

02 Nutrition & digestion
Human study

Daily intake of broccoli sprouts normalizes bowel habits in human healthy subjects

Published research · 2018 · Free full text via PubMed Central

A small human study explored what happened when healthy adults ate broccoli sprouts daily for four weeks. The paper also includes a nutrition table showing components such as vitamin C, vitamin K, protein, calcium, iron and dietary fibre.

03 Sulforaphane
Research review

Exploring the anti-inflammatory activity of sulforaphane

Treasure, Harris & Williamson · Immunology & Cell Biology · 2023

This scientific review brings together research into how sulforaphane interacts with cellular pathways linked to inflammation and the body's antioxidant response. It also describes questions scientists are continuing to investigate.
